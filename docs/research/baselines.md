# Baseline policy

DDPG is the first implementation and verification gate. The project-owned standard DDPG baseline now has online and target actor/critic networks, replay TD regression, deterministic policy gradients, collection-only Gaussian noise, and Polyak target updates. Focused tests verify its contracts; broader multi-seed empirical validation remains future work. Standard DDPG is the primary four-network comparison. The implemented `ddpg_no_target_actor` controlled ablation retains the online actor, online critic, and target critic; it uses the detached online actor at the next state for critic bootstrapping and soft-updates only the target critic. It isolates removal of the target actor and remains experimental until controlled multi-seed results exist. TD3 is the next strong direct continuous-control baseline.

SAC is a useful later continuous-control reference, but its stochastic policy and entropy objective change the assumptions. A2C and A3C are later on-policy contextual baselines, not architecturally equivalent to DDPG. A3C requires a separate multiprocessing design and must not be inserted as a trivial off-policy trainer variant.

Do not vendor a baseline in this stage. Later, either implement a clean, tested version under the common interface or build a thin adapter to a maintained library for verification. Any copied or adapted code needs source URL, exact commit or version, license, and local modification record in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` before inclusion.
